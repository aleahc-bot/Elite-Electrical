Frequently Asked Commercial Electrical Questions

What commercial electrical services do you provide in Southwest Florida?

As a Florida state-certified electrical contractor (License #EC13003980), we handle three-phase power distribution, tenant improvements, commercial lighting design and retrofits, main panel upgrades, dedicated equipment circuits, preventative maintenance, and emergency commercial repairs.

Can you handle tenant improvements and retail/office build-outs?

Yes. We work closely with general contractors, business owners, and property managers across Lee and Collier counties to complete turnkey commercial electrical build-outs on time and within budget.

Do you offer scheduled preventative maintenance for commercial facilities?

Yes. Preventative electrical inspections and maintenance help identify thermal hotspots, failing breakers, and balance circuit loads before costly equipment failure or operational downtime occurs.

How do you minimize operational downtime during electrical upgrades?

We schedule heavy switchgear cutovers, panel shutdowns, and major wiring installations during off-peak hours or weekends to keep your daily business operations running without interruption.

Are your electricians certified for three-phase power and high-voltage industrial work?

Yes. Our licensed electricians (License #EC13003980) are fully trained and certified to safely service and install 120/208V, 277/480V, and specialized commercial and industrial three-phase power systems.

Can you assist commercial properties with energy-efficient LED retrofits?

Absolutely. Upgrading warehouse, parking lot, exterior security, and office lighting to commercial LEDs drastically reduces utility costs and HVAC heat load while noticeably improving facility visibility.
