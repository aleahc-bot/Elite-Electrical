Frequently Asked Electrical Repairs & Service Questions

What are the most common electrical repairs you handle?

As a Florida state-certified electrical contractor (License #EC13003980), we frequently diagnose and repair dead outlets, broken light switches, buzzing circuit breakers, burning smells, flickering fixtures, GFCI receptacle failures, and storm-related electrical surge damage.

What should I do if an outlet is sparking, buzzing, or warm to the touch?

Turn off power to that circuit at your main breaker panel immediately and leave the fixture alone. Book an emergency service visit right away, as warm, buzzing, or sparking outlets indicate loose arcing connections and are common household fire hazards.

Why does my circuit breaker keep tripping when running appliances?

Repeatedly tripping breakers indicate an overloaded branch circuit, a direct short circuit, or a dangerous ground fault. If resetting the breaker causes it to trip again immediately, do not force the switch to stay on; call our licensed electricians (License #EC13003980) to test the line load and circuit integrity.

How do you diagnose hidden wiring problems or intermittent power loss?

Our technicians use digital multimeters, thermal imaging infrared cameras, and tone-generator circuit tracers to locate hidden wire breaks, corroded splices, loose neutral lines, or overheated junction boxes concealed inside walls and attics.

Is it safe for homeowners to perform DIY electrical repairs?

While swapping a decorative faceplate is safe, working on active wiring, outlets, switches, and service panels carries severe electrocution and structural fire risks. Always leave line-voltage diagnostic and repair work to licensed, insured professionals (License #EC13003980).
