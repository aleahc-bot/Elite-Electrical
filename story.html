Our Journey

Built on Trust.
Driven by People.

Since 2001.

From a spare bedroom and single garage bay to a leading electrical contractor in Southwest Florida, our story has always been built on hard work, lasting relationships, and a belief that taking care of our people is the foundation of our success.

In September 2001, three friends set out to build an electrical contracting company that would do things differently.

Chipper Fitz, Shannon Fitz and Andy Forgione wanted a workplace where employees felt valued, where customers received personal attention, and where quality workmanship was never compromised.

That people-first philosophy became the foundation of Elite’s culture and a driving force behind its success. More than two decades later, it still shapes how the company treats its employees, serves its customers and approaches every project.

How we grew

From humble beginnings to a Southwest Florida leader.

Chipper and Andy came up in the trade at the same time. Both completed a four-year, state-certified apprenticeship, and both spent years working together on high-rises, schools, medical facilities and commercial projects across Southwest Florida before they ever had a company of their own.

2001

A spare bedroom and single car garage bay

Chip Fitz, Shannon Fitz and Andy Forgione launched Elite from a spare bedroom in Chip and Shannon’s Lehigh Acres home, using a single garage bay as their warehouse. Their first customer was Faith Assembly Church of God in North Fort Myers, where they worked side by side for nearly two years.

2003

Thirty people, thirteen trucks

By keeping their promises and showing up on time, they earned the trust of customers across Southwest Florida. Within two years, the company grew to about 30 employees and 13 trucks.

Metro Parkway

A larger facility and $15 million in annual sales

As Elite continued to grow, the company moved to a larger facility on Metro Parkway in Fort Myers, eventually expanding to 125 employees and more than $15 million in annual sales.

2007

10,777 square feet on Unity Avenue

Elite moved into a newly remodeled 10,777-square-foot facility on Unity Avenue — the new facility gave Elite the space and resources to take on bigger projects and better serve Southwest Florida.

The build years

Residential, commercial and industrial

As Southwest Florida expanded, so did Elite. The company broadened across residential, commercial and industrial work — new construction, renovation and service — and added air conditioning and refrigeration so it could serve even more customers in the region.

2019

A new state-of-the-art 30,000-square-foot facility on Laredo Avenue

By 2019, Elite had grown to more than 160 employees and 60 service trucks, prompting a move to our new, state-of-the-art 30,000-square-foot facility to support continued growth and better serve our customers. It was a remarkable transformation from our beginnings in a spare-bedroom office with a single garage bay for a warehouse.

2024–Present

Proudly employee-owned

After nearly 25 years of building Elite, our founders made a decision that would shape its future: putting ownership in the hands of the employees who helped make it successful.

On December 31, 2024, Elite became 100% employee-owned through an ESOP, with over 220 employees and 80 vehicles. The founders remain actively involved, preserving the culture that built our success: Employees First. Family First. Customers Second.

What hasn’t changed

Our commitment remains the same.

Through every stage of growth, Chipper, Shannon and Andy have stayed with the principles that made them start Elite in the first place. They built a company where employees are recognised, customers are respected, and relationships matter as much as the work.

What began as three friends with a vision is now one of Southwest Florida’s established electrical contracting companies — built on hard work, lasting relationships and a habit of keeping promises.
