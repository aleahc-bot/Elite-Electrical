LOGO FIX — 6 files, repo root
====================================================================

WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------
On the services pages and the contact page the logo was invisible at
the top of the page and only appeared once you scrolled down.

My fault, and here is the reason. Those pages were originally built
with TWO logo images: the colour one, hidden while the header sits
over the dark banner, and a white one shown in its place - swapped
over as you scroll. The page's own stylesheet still carried the rule
that does the hiding:

    header:not(.scrolled) .brand .l-navy{opacity:0}

When I replaced the two images with the single new mark, that rule
was left behind with nothing to swap to. So the only logo on the page
was being hidden until you scrolled.

Fixed by overriding it so the one mark stays visible in both states.


AND THE LOGO IS NOW FULL SIZE ON THESE PAGES TOO
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Same root cause. These pages carry their own copy of the old header -
an 84px bar with a 52px logo. The rest of the site moved to a 136px
bar with a 124px mark some time ago, but that change lived in the
shared stylesheet, which these pages never load. So the logo here was
less than half the size it is on the homepage.

They now step through exactly the same sizes as every other page:

    desktop        bar 136px   logo 124px
    <= 1180px      bar 132px   logo 106px
    <= 430px       bar 126px   logo  98px
    <= 340px       bar 108px   logo  82px

The mobile menu offsets moved with it - pinned to the old 84px the
menu would have opened underneath the header - and the banner gained
the breathing room a taller bar needs, so no heading tucks under it.
