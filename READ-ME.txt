WHAT CHANGED
=====================================================================

1. THE LOGO ON THE SERVICES AND CONTACT PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
It was invisible until you scrolled, and half the size it is on the
homepage. Both had the same cause: those pages carry their own copy of
the old header and never load the shared stylesheet.

  * The old design had TWO logos - colour, hidden over the dark
    banner, and a white one shown instead. The rule that hides the
    colour one was still there with nothing left to swap to, so the
    only logo on the page was hidden until you scrolled.
  * The bar is now 136px with a 124px mark, stepping down to 126/98
    on a phone, exactly as on every other page. The mobile menu
    offsets and the banner padding moved with it.


2. BLUE ON BLUE, EVERYWHERE IT HAPPENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
I had only fixed the Electrical card the first time. This pass went
through every accent on these pages that lands on a dark ground.

  THE DARK ELECTRICAL CARD
    the POWER line, the four tick chips, the bolt tile - all accent
    blue on a blue panel, about 3:1. Chrome silver now, 11.5:1 on the
    same navy, and the tick chips read as actually ticked.

  THE PAGE BANNER EYEBROW   <- the one you spotted
    "WHAT WE DO" and "CONTACT" were bright blue on the dark banner.
    On New Construction and Our Story that same eyebrow is silver,
    because those pages load the shared stylesheet. These pages do
    not, so they never got it. They match now.

  THE SERVICE CARD ICONS
    the 01-05 cards sit on white, but their icon tile is a navy
    gradient - so a blue glyph on it is the same problem one layer
    down. White glyph now.

  AND THE REST
    the careers band eyebrow, the ESOP panel's bold text and badge,
    the footer column headings and the footer's serif tag line. All
    the same set css/site.css applies to the other 25 pages.

  Contact details icons: blue glyph on a blue tile. White now.


3. "THANK YOU FOR SUBMITTING" NO LONGER SITS UNDER THE FORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------
That panel was always meant to appear only after a request is sent,
and the markup had it marked hidden. But the page's own stylesheet set
display:flex on it, which outranks the browser's rule for the hidden
attribute - so it showed from the moment the page loaded. Pinned shut
now. It still appears when the form is actually submitted.

5. SCROLL ANIMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"How a job goes" - the four steps used to fade up all at once, which
on a wide screen means you never see it happen. They now arrive one
after another over about 1.4 seconds, each rising 40px, with the
numbered disc scaling in a beat behind its own step.

The Our Story timeline - the rail draws itself down the gutter first,
then each dot pops onto it, then the milestones cascade in behind.
About a second and a half end to end.

Both stop completely for anyone whose device asks for reduced motion.
