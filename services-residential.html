Frequently Asked Residential Electrical Questions

What residential electrical services do you specialize in?

As a state-certified electrical contractor (License #EC13003980), we specialize in electrical panel upgrades, Level 2 EV charger installations, custom lighting design, remodel wiring, smart home automation, and general electrical troubleshooting.

How do I know if my home’s electrical wiring is safe or needs an update?

If your home was built before 1990, or you notice buzzing outlets, flickering lights, burning smells, or frequently tripping breakers, your electrical system needs evaluation. Our licensed electricians (License #EC13003980) perform comprehensive safety inspections to ensure your wiring meets current National Electrical Code standards.

Can you install electric vehicle (EV) chargers in residential homes?

Yes, we install dedicated 240V Level 2 EV charging stations for Tesla, Rivian, Ford, and all universal J1772-compatible electric vehicles, including dedicated circuit runs and panel load capacity checks.

What should I do if my circuit breakers keep tripping?

A repeatedly tripping breaker signals an overloaded circuit, a short circuit, or a dangerous ground fault. Never force the breaker handle to stay in the “ON” position. Book a diagnostic repair visit online to have our licensed technicians identify and resolve the issue safely.
