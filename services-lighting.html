Frequently Asked Electrical Lighting Services Questions

Do you install both interior lighting and exterior landscape lighting?

Yes. As a Florida state-certified electrical contractor (License #EC13003980), we handle full interior lighting layouts (recessed wafer lights, pendant fixtures, chandeliers, track lighting, and sconces) as well as low-voltage exterior landscape, pathway, and architectural facade lighting.

Can you install custom lighting for lanais, pool cages, and boat docks?

Yes. We specialize in weather-rated outdoor illumination designed for coastal environments, including color-changing LED pool cage nebula lighting, underwater dock lights, piling lights, and lanai ceiling fan/light combos.

What are the benefits of upgrading older fixtures to LED?

LED lighting uses up to 80% less energy, emits far less heat (which helps lower indoor air conditioning costs during Florida summers), and lasts up to 25 times longer than traditional incandescent or halogen bulbs.

Can you add recessed can lighting in rooms that currently have no overhead light?

Yes. Our licensed electricians (License #EC13003980) cut clean precision openings, fish wiring through attics or ceiling joists, and install energy-efficient ultra-thin wafer LED lights with integrated junction boxes and minimal drywall disturbance.

Do you install smart home lighting controls and automated dimmers?

Yes. We install and configure smart dimmers, motion sensors, scene switches, and automated timers compatible with systems such as Lutron Caséta, Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa.

What should I consider when selecting security and flood lighting?

High-efficiency motion-activated LED floodlights provide superior security without unnecessary light pollution or blinding neighbors. We help position fixtures to eliminate blind spots around driveways, dark side yards, and entryways while ensuring weather-sealed durability.
