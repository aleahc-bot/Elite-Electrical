Built to Code. Delivered on Schedule.

New construction demands an electrical contractor who respects trade sequencing, job site timelines, and rigid building inspection schedules. Elite Electrical Contractors brings over two decades of Southwest Florida field experience to every build. As an employee-owned company, our electricians have a direct stake in delivering clean, code-compliant, and dependable installations on every job site.

Whether you are building a custom waterfront estate in Cape Coral, a multi-family community in Fort Myers, or a commercial development in Naples, we coordinate directly with your project superintendents, architects, and mechanical trades to eliminate rework and prevent costly inspection delays.

Our End-to-End Construction Process

  • Pre-Construction & Underground Utility Work: Comprehensive blueprint reviews, load calculations, temporary construction power poles, underground conduit trenching, grounding grids, and coordination with local utilities (FPL and LCEC).
  • Precision Rough-In Phase: Branch circuit wiring, panel rough-ins, dedicated equipment lines, HVAC disconnects, structured low-voltage cabling, EV charging conduits, and automatic generator transfer switch pre-wiring.
  • Fixture Trim-Out & Finish Work: Architectural lighting installation, recessed LED wafer placement, chandelier lifts, custom dimming packages, commercial switchgear terminations, and whole-structure surge protection.
  • Inspections & Final Sign-Off: Full system walk-throughs and circuit continuity testing to ensure 100% compliance with Florida Building Code and National Electrical Code (NEC) standards before municipal building officials arrive.

Engineered for Southwest Florida’s Coastal Climate

Building in Southwest Florida presents environmental demands that standard electrical designs cannot handle:

  • Surge Protection: Whole-home and commercial Type 1 and Type 2 surge suppression to shield appliances, HVAC systems, and automation panels from severe storm-induced grid spikes.
  • Corrosion Resistance: Weather-rated NEMA 3R and 4X disconnects, marine-grade conduit fittings, and stainless steel hardware engineered for pools, lanais, docks, and coastal salt air.
  • Backup Power Integration: Pre-wiring for standby generators, automatic transfer switches, and critical load panels during framing.
  • Energy Code Compliance: High-efficiency LED layouts, occupancy sensors, and smart dimming systems built to meet Florida Energy Conservation codes.

Frequently Asked New Construction Electrical Questions

Do you partner directly with general contractors and custom home builders?

Yes. As a state-certified electrical contractor (License #EC13003980), we partner directly with custom home builders, residential developers, and commercial general contractors throughout Lee, Collier, and Charlotte counties.

At what stage of construction should an electrical contractor be brought in?

Consulting during the architectural and pre-construction design phase is ideal. Bringing in our licensed team (License #EC13003980) early allows us to calculate service load requirements, design conduit paths, coordinate with FPL or LCEC for utility drops, and prevent costly framing alterations later.

Do you manage the electrical permitting and inspection process?

Yes. We pull all required electrical permits under Florida License #EC13003980 across local jurisdictions—including Lee County, Collier County, Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Naples—and handle all rough and final inspections directly with building officials.

Can Elite design custom electrical layouts for high-end residential builds?

Yes. We work from architectural drawings to build custom electrical layouts for high-end homes, including integrated lanai entertainment hubs, recessed ceiling lights, smart automation panels, EV charging ports, and whole-home backup generator systems.

What is the typical timeline for rough-in and trim-out phases?

Electrical rough-in begins immediately after the building shell is dried-in and plumbing/HVAC rough-ins are set, usually taking 1 to 2 weeks depending on square footage. Trim-out takes place after drywall and paint are finished, taking 3 to 7 days before final inspection sign-off.

How do you ensure all new construction wiring meets Florida Building Code?

Our licensed electricians (License #EC13003980) build strictly to the National Electrical Code (NEC) and current Florida Building Code standards. Every circuit is tested for arc-fault, ground-fault, and surge protection compliance before any inspection is called.
